AI for Ranking-based Optimization
1. Find a (quantitative) problem that you like. (e.g., molecule solubility, airfoil shape). 
Criteria:
a. 1-D problem, quantitative problem. We can quantify the performance of a design with a single 
scalar number.
b. We have computational tools to verify the performance of a new design.
2. Implement and evaluate LLM as,
a. A regression surrogate, i.e., predicting the absolute performance of each design
b. A pairwise ranker surrogate, i.e., predicting the relative performance between design A and design 
B (is A better than B).
3. Implement and evaluate optimization framework:
a. LLM as a direct optimizer
b. Bayesian Optimization with LLM as a surrogate regressor
c. Preferential Bayesian Optimization with LLM as a surrogate pairwise ranker
